## Challenge

An e-commerce company needed to scale their platform to handle 10x traffic during Black Friday and holiday sales. The existing infrastructure couldn't handle the traffic spikes, resulting in downtime and lost revenue.

## Approach

We implemented auto-scaling cloud infrastructure, optimized database queries, and added CDN caching. We also implemented load testing and monitoring to ensure the platform could handle peak traffic.

## Results

- **Zero downtime** during peak sales events
- **10x traffic capacity** achieved
- **40% reduction** in page load times
- **25% increase** in conversion rates
